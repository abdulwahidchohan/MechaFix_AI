# Circuit Inspection Photo Capture Guidelines

## Best Practices for AI Visual Diagnosis
1. **Perpendicular Top-Down Overhead View**: Position camera directly above the breadboard or PCB to clearly reveal component silkscreen labels and pin rows.
2. **Bright Even Lighting**: Ensure adequate lighting to avoid harsh shadows over IC part numbers and resistor color bands.
3. **Trace Clearance**: Spread jumper wires so IC part numbers (e.g. L298N, ATmega328P, ESP32-WROOM) and module headers (VCC, GND, Trig, Echo, SDA, SCL) remain unobstructed.
4. **Focus On IC Markings**: Ensure camera focus is sharp on IC chips and pin header silkscreen text.
