# Hardware Electrical Safety & Emergency Protocols

## High Risk Electrical Hazards
1. **Smoke, Sparks, or Burning Smell**: Immediately disconnect the main DC power source or unplug USB cables. Do NOT touch components until power is removed and thermal dissipation occurs.
2. **Swollen LiPo / Lithium Batteries**: Disconnect immediately if safe. Place battery in a fireproof container or sand bucket. NEVER puncture or attempt to charge a swollen cell.
3. **Exposed High-Voltage Mains (110V - 240V AC)**: Do NOT attempt procedural repairs on bare AC lines or open transformer windings. Always consult a licensed electrician or certified technician.
4. **Short Circuits**: Always measure resistance between VCC and GND with power off before connecting power for the first time. Zero ohms indicates a short circuit that will damage regulators or USB ports.
